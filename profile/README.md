### Deception that keeps you safe from AI attack agents.

AI agents now attack websites on their own. They scan, probe, and try exploits around the clock, faster and cheaper than any human attacker.

Counterprompt fights back with deception. It plants decoys that people never see and well-behaved search engines skip. When an attack agent goes for one, Counterprompt quietly feeds it misleading information. The agent burns its budget on dead ends and reports results that are wrong.

- **Real visitors are unaffected.** Counterprompt responds only to bots that go after the decoys.
- **Defensive only.** It shapes your own site's responses and never attacks anyone back.
- **Tested in the lab** against real AI attack agents.
